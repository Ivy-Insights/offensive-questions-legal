Privacy Policy

Effective: June 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Offensive Questions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Offensive Questions," "we," "us," or "our"), operates the offensivequestions.com website and the Offensive Questions iOS mobile application (together, the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Service and to our related communications. It does not apply to third-party services we link to or interoperate with, which have their own policies.

We are the controller of the personal information described here.

The Service is offered only in the United States and Canada. This Policy is written for users located in those two countries.

The Service is for adults only. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. See Section 10.

The Service is currently available on iOS via the Apple App Store.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Account information

Mobile phone number (used to sign in via a one-time passcode sent by text message), display name, avatar, and, optionally, full name, sex, and date of birth if you add them to your profile.

Guest information

If you play as a guest, a display name, an avatar, and a device identifier, without a phone number, unless and until you convert to a full account.

Purchase and order information

Records of subscriptions and in-app purchases, transaction identifiers, receipt validation data; for merchandise orders, your billing name, billing address, shipping name, shipping address, phone number, and email address. We do not receive or store your full payment card number for either in-app or merchandise purchases — those are processed by Apple or by our payment processor.

Gameplay content

Spoken or written answers to game prompts, which may be recorded as audio, along with an automatically generated waveform; text and voice chat messages sent to other players in a game; ratings you give to questions; profile photos or avatar images you upload.

Communications

Messages you send to support through our in-app contact form, feedback, survey responses, and bug reports.

Reports and blocks

Reports you submit about another player or content, and players you block.

Consent records

The date, version, and (where applicable) IP address and browser/device identifier associated with your acceptance of these Terms and this Policy.

Marketing preferences

Your subscription status and timezone for push notifications and similar communications.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Device information

Device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen dimensions, app version.

Identifiers

Device identifiers, installation identifiers, push notification token, IP address.

Usage data

Sessions, screens viewed, features used, decks or packs opened, gameplay events, taps, session duration, referral and attribution data.

Diagnostics

Crash logs, error reports, performance data.

Approximate location

Coarse location inferred from your IP address, typically at the country or region level. We do not collect precise GPS location.

Camera

If you use the in-app QR code scanner to join a game on another device, the app accesses your camera to read the code. We do not store photos or video captured this way.

Microphone and audio

If you record a spoken answer or a voice chat message during gameplay, we access your microphone and store the resulting audio recording and its waveform as described in Section 2.1.

Cookies and similar technologies

On our website: cookies, local storage, and pixels. In the app: SDKs and local storage. See Section 8.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Apple. Apple provides us with purchase and subscription status, refund and cancellation events, and aggregate download and performance data for App Store transactions.
  • Our payment processor. For merchandise purchases, our payment processor shares payment status, order, billing, and shipping information with us.
  • Our fulfillment partner. Our print-on-demand merchandise partner shares order and shipment status.
  • Analytics providers. Aggregated and device-level usage and install-source data.

2.4 Sensitive Information

Your profile may optionally include your sex, which we treat as sensitive personal information under some state and Canadian privacy laws. We do not otherwise intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as government identifiers, financial account numbers, precise geolocation, biometric data, health information, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or sexual orientation, and we ask that you not submit such information through User Content or support messages beyond what this Section describes.

Your recorded voice answers and voice chat messages are audio recordings, not biometric identifiers: we do not use them to identify you through voiceprint or other biometric analysis.

We do not sell sensitive personal data. We do not sell biometric personal data.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as described below.

Service providers. We use vendors that process personal information on our behalf, under contract and only on our instructions, including:

  • Railway — cloud application hosting and database infrastructure
  • Google Firebase Authentication — sign-in session and identity token issuance (paired with Twilio for the underlying SMS one-time passcode)

Apple. Apple processes your in-app purchases and subscriptions and provides us with related transaction data.

Other players. Your display name, avatar, and any answer, rating, or chat message (including a voice message) you submit during a game are visible to the other players in that game. Do not submit anything you would not want seen or heard by the people you are playing with.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms; to detect or address fraud, security, or technical issues; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Offensive Questions, our users, or the public.

Corporate transactions. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy or a successor policy.

With your direction or consent. For any other purpose you approve.

Aggregated or de-identified data. We may create and use aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you, for any purpose, and we will not attempt to reidentify it.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • Account information: for as long as your account is active, and then deleted or de-identified within 30 days of account deletion, except where we must retain it longer.
  • Merchandise order records: as required for tax, accounting, audit, and dispute resolution purposes, typically 7 years.
  • Usage and analytics data: typically up to 25 months, in aggregated or de-identified form thereafter.
  • Support communications: typically up to 3 years after the matter is resolved.
  • Data retained for legal reasons: for as long as needed to comply with law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted data may persist in backups for a limited period before being purged.

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights:

  • Access and portability: to know what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy in a portable format.
  • Correction: to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Deletion: to request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out: of the sale or sharing of personal information, of targeted advertising, and of certain profiling.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.
  • Object to or restrict processing, where applicable law provides that right.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised a privacy right.
  • Complain to your state attorney general, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to your provincial privacy regulator.

How to Exercise Your Rights

  • In the app: you can delete your account, along with the account data described in this Policy, from the account settings screen.
  • By email: to request an export of your account data or to exercise any other right described above, contact support@offensivequestions.com with your request and the phone number associated with your account.

We will verify your identity before acting, typically by confirming control of your account's phone number. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.

We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, generally 45 days under U.S. state privacy laws (extendable by another 45 days with notice) and 30 days under Canadian privacy law.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or writing to support@offensivequestions.com with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond within 60 days. If we deny your appeal, you may contact your state attorney general.
