Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the app, sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used originally, then tap Restore Purchases from the paywall screen or Profile → Settings. Your VIP status will be re-granted within a few seconds.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, find Offensive Questions, and tap Cancel Subscription. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it via Apple Settings before deleting your account.

The game won't connect or join a room

  • Check that your device is online (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Make sure the game code is entered correctly — codes are case-insensitive.
  • Try force-quitting and reopening the app.
  • If the problem persists, email support@offensivequestions.com with the game code and a short description of what you saw.

I have a billing issue

For purchases made through the App Store (subscriptions), Apple handles all billing inquiries. You can request a refund directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. For any other billing question, email support@offensivequestions.com.

I want to report inappropriate content or a user

Tap and hold the offending message or open the user's profile and use the in-app Report or Block controls. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team and acted on within 24 hours in accordance with our zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content.
